# MOMOBLOBS — 30 Games Plan (Telugu)

Prathi game lo: **funny clay characters · levels ki end ledu (unlimited) · score + combo + best score · coins · phone, tablet, PC anni lo pani chestundi.**
Focus and memory games ki ekkuva importance (30 lo 14 games). Migatavi brain, speed, numbers, puzzle — chinna pillalu nunchi peddala varaku andariki nachela.

## Anni games ki common rules

1. **Levels ki end ledu.** Prathi level computer automatic ga kotha ga create chestundi, so okate level malli malli raadu. Difficulty konchem konchem perugutundi — sudden ga kastam avvadu. Speed full ayyaka, kotha twists add avutayi.
2. **Rendu rakala games:**
   - **Round games** (focus, memory, brain, numbers, puzzle): **game over ledu.** Miss aithe same level kotha layout tho malli vastundi. Score add avutune untundi.
   - **Run games** (4 speed games): **3 hearts.** Tappu chesthe okka heart pothundi. 3 hearts aipothe score chupistundi, ventane malli start cheyyachu.
3. **Score:** correct ki points × **combo** (varusaga correct chesthe ×2, ×3 … ×5 varaku perugutundi) + fast ga chesthe **speed bonus**. Miss aithe combo malli ×1. **Best score**, max level save avutayi.
4. **Coins:** prathi level clear chesina/run aipoyina tarvata coins vastayi → cup shop lo vaadukovachu.
5. **Funny feedback:** characters expressions maarutayi, squash-stretch jumps, confetti, original clay sounds (boing, pop, squish, Kranky navvu, tada, sad trombone).

# 🎯 FOCUS GAMES (7)

### 1. Fish Watch — Pip
**Katha:** Pip ki aakali. Pond lo konni golden fish unnayi, kaani Pip vaati ni marchipoyadu. Meeru gurthu pettukovali!
**Ela aadali:**
1. Modata konni fish **golden** ga merustayi (2 seconds).
2. Tarvata anni fish okate grey colour loki maari pond lo tirugutayi.
3. Fish aagipoyaka, golden ga unna fish ni anni tap cheyyali.
**Levels:** golden fish 1 → 6 varaku, mottam fish 4 → 18, swimming time 3 → 9 seconds, speed perugutundi. Pai levels lo fish okadanini okati cross chesukuntu confuse chestayi.
**Score:** prathi correct fish ki 100 points, anni correct aithe **perfect bonus**.
**Funny:** correct fish dorikithe Pip noru teruchukuni gulp chestadu. Wrong fish tap chesthe aa fish Pip mukham meeda tail tho kodutundi 🐟💥
**Nerchukunedi:** okkasari chala vastuvulni track cheyyadam (multiple object tracking).

### 2. Spot the Sneak — Gloop
**Katha:** Gloop gang lo okadu dongha. Andaru same ga kanipistaru, kaani okadu konchem different.
**Ela aadali:** Gumpu lo different ga unna Gloop ni tap cheyyali — kannu, pannu, colour, leda tala tilt different ga untundi. Timer bar karugutundi (melting slime).
**Levels:** gumpu 6 → 64 Gloops, difference chinnadi avutundi, pai levels lo gumpu antha wobble avutundi.
**Score:** entha fast ga pattukunte anni points. Wrong tap chesthe timer konchem karugutundi.
**Funny:** dongha Gloop pattubadithe "uh-oh" face tho split ayyi paripotadu.
**Nerchukunedi:** visual attention, chinna differences gamaninchadam.

### 3. Color Clash — Prof. Hoot
**Katha:** Hoot sir class lo colours test pedtunnaru… kaani words ki, colours ki match avvadam ledu!
**Ela aadali:** Screen meeda word vastundi — udaharanaki **"RED"** ane word **blue** colour lo raasi untundi. Paina board cheptundi:
- "Tap the **INK**" → word raasina colour (blue) button tap cheyyali.
- "Tap the **WORD**" → word cheppina colour (red) tap cheyyali.
**Levels:** prathi level lo 10 questions. Time taggutundi, rule tondaraga maarutundi, colours perugutayi.
**Score:** correct × combo + speed bonus.
**Funny:** meeru wrong chesthe Hoot sir glasses kinda jaripoyi nidrapotaru 😴
**Nerchukunedi:** focus control, brain confuse ayina correct ga decide cheyyadam (Stroop test).

### 4. Blink! — Noodle
**Katha:** Noodle room choodandi. Noodle kannu moosi terichesariki edo maaripoyindi!
**Ela aadali:**
1. Room lo vastuvulu kontha sepu chudandi.
2. Noodle "blink" chestadu (screen okka kshanam close avutundi).
3. Okati maarutundi — colour maarachu, kanipinchakapovachu, place maarachu. Edi maarindo tap cheyyali.
**Levels:** vastuvulu 4 → 24, changes chinnavi avutayi, choose time taggutundi, pai levels lo okesari 2 changes.
**Score:** correct ki points + fast ga chusthe bonus.
**Funny:** Noodle chala podugu kabatti room lo pattadu — tail okka side nunchi, tala inko side nunchi vastayi.
**Nerchukunedi:** detail memory, gamaninchadam.

### 5. Where's Zib? — Zib
**Katha:** Zib alien festival lo daakkunnadu. Andaru aliens ki rendu kallu — Zib ki okate kannu + antenna.
**Ela aadali:** Busy scene lo Zib ni vethiki tap cheyyali.
**Levels:** gumpu 10 → 120 varaku, Zib laage unna look-alike aliens, pai levels lo gumpu kadulutundi.
**Score:** entha fast ga dorikithe anni points.
**Funny:** Zib dorikithe UFO lo paripodaniki try chestadu, kaani UFO crash avutundi 🛸
**Nerchukunedi:** visual search, patience.

### 6. Shadow Snap — Noodle
**Katha:** Noodle shadow puppets show. Ee shadow evaridi?
**Ela aadali:** Oka clay shadow vastundi. Kinda unna characters lo aa shadow evaridho tap cheyyali.
**Levels:** options 3 → 9, shadows tirigi (rotate), mirror (flip) ga vastayi, time taggutundi.
**Score:** correct × combo.
**Funny:** wrong aithe aa character shadow nunchi bayataki vachi "nenu kaadu!" ani gola chestundi.
**Nerchukunedi:** shapes, rotation ardham chesukovadam.

### 7. Gloop Guard — Gloop *(run game, 3 hearts)*
**Katha:** Meeru party door guard. Green Gloops friends — lopaliki rani. Red Gloops troublemakers — aapali!
**Ela aadali:** Door daggara Gloops vastaru. **Green** vallani tap chesi lopaliki pampandi. **Red** vallani, Momo ni asalu tap cheyyakandi.
**Levels:** Gloops tondaraga vastaru, colour maarche Gloops, look-alikes vastaru.
**Score:** prathi correct ki 10 × combo. Red ni tap chesthe heart pothundi.
**Funny:** red Gloop lopaliki vasthe party cake meeda padi antha sticky chestadu.
**Nerchukunedi:** self-control — "aagu" ani decide cheyyadam (go / no-go).

# 🧠 MEMORY GAMES (7)

### 8. Memory Bridge — Tiki
**Katha:** Tiki river daatali. Konni stones maatrame safe — migatavi neellalo munigipotayi!
**Ela aadali:** Stones ee order lo merustayi → same order lo tap chesi Tiki ni hop cheyyinchali. Tappu stone aithe SPLASH!
**Levels:** path 3 stones nunchi start, prathi level ki +1 (limit ledu). Grid 3×3 → 5×6, flash speed perugutundi.
**Score:** prathi stone ki 50 × combo.
**Funny:** splash ayyaka Tiki mukham meeda chepa tho bayataki vastundi.
**Nerchukunedi:** order memory.

### 9. Pip's Pantry — Pip
**Katha:** Pip fridge lo food petti, ekkada pettado marchipoyadu.
**Ela aadali:** Fridge doors open chesi same food pairs (2 ice cream, 2 fish…) ni match cheyyali.
**Levels:** pairs 3 → 18. Pai levels lo Kranky vachi moosi unna rendu doors ni maarchestadu!
**Score:** prathi match ki points, takkuva moves lo aipothe bonus.
**Funny:** match aina food ni Pip ventane tinesthadu, tarvata "nenu em tinna?" ani adugutadu.
**Nerchukunedi:** place memory (ekkada undo gurthu).

### 10. Bolt's Beep Boop — Bolt
**Katha:** Bolt robot ki kotha dance kavali. Beeps gurthu pettukunte ne dance chestadu.
**Ela aadali:** Bolt chest meeda colour buttons oka order lo beep tho merustayi → meeru same order lo tap cheyyali.
**Levels:** prathi level ki +1 beep, buttons 4 → 6. Prathi 5th level **"Backwards!"** — reverse order lo tap cheyyali.
**Score:** prathi beep ki 20 × level.
**Funny:** correct aithe Bolt robot dance chestadu, wrong aithe smoke vachi head spin avutundi.
**Nerchukunedi:** sound + colour sequence memory.

### 11. Order Up! — Bonbon
**Katha:** Bonbon cake shop. Customers order chesi vellipotaru — gurthu pettukuni cake cheyyali.
**Ela aadali:** Order card konni seconds chupistundi (chocolate layer, strawberry layer, cherry topping…). Card moosaka, kinda nunchi paiki same order lo layers tap cheyyali.
**Levels:** layers 2 → 8, toppings add avutayi, pai levels lo okesari 2 orders.
**Score:** prathi correct layer ki points + perfect cake bonus.
**Funny:** tappu chesthe Bonbon aa cake ni okka gulp lo tinesthundi.
**Nerchukunedi:** list memory, order.

### 12. Glow Grid — Bolt
**Katha:** Bolt screen lo lights blink avutunnayi — repair cheyyali.
**Ela aadali:** Grid lo konni boxes konchem sepu merustayi → aarina tarvata merisina boxes anni tap cheyyali. Pai levels lo **merisina order lo** tap cheyyali.
**Levels:** grid 3×3 → 7×7, boxes 3 → 18, glow time taggutundi.
**Score:** prathi box ki points, perfect bonus.
**Funny:** wrong aithe Bolt screen lo "ERROR 404: BRAIN NOT FOUND" ani vastundi 🤖
**Nerchukunedi:** visual pattern memory.

### 13. Who Said It? — andaru characters
**Katha:** Stage meeda characters funny sounds chestunnaru — evaru mundu, evaru tarvata?
**Ela aadali:** Characters okkokkaru pop ayyi funny sound chestaru (boing, pop, Kranky navvu, Bolt beep…). Tarvata same order lo characters ni tap cheyyali.
**Levels:** sequence perugutundi, stage meeda characters perugutaru, seats maaripotayi.
**Score:** prathi correct ki points × combo.
**Funny:** characters sounds chaala silly — burps, honks, squeaks.
**Nerchukunedi:** listening memory (sound tho gurthu).

### 14. What's Missing? — Kranky
**Katha:** Kranky dongha! Shelf nunchi edo dongilinchadu.
**Ela aadali:** Shelf lo vastuvulu chudandi → curtain mooskuntundi → Kranky okati teesukuntadu → curtain terichaka emi missing o choices nunchi tap cheyyali.
**Levels:** vastuvulu 4 → 20, choose time taggutundi, Kranky 1 → 3 vastuvulu dongilistadu.
**Score:** correct × combo.
**Funny:** correct aithe Kranky dongilinchina vastuvu tana top hat lo nunchi bayataki padutundi 🎩
**Nerchukunedi:** short-term memory.

# 💡 BRAIN / LOGIC GAMES (4)

### 15. Pick the Door — Kranky
**Katha:** Kranky prize ni okka door venaka daachadu. Clues isthadu — kaani clues chaala tricky!
**Ela aadali:** Konni doors (colours, symbols) untayi. Kranky clues: "Red kaadu", "Star door ki left side", "Middle lo ledu"… Clues tho correct door tap cheyyali.
**Levels:** doors 3 → 8, kotha clue types, kashtamaina combinations.
**Score:** correct ki points, takkuva clues chusi correct chesthe bonus.
**Funny:** wrong door lo nunchi rubber chicken leda water bucket padutundi.
**Nerchukunedi:** logical thinking.

### 16. Pattern Parade — Prof. Hoot
**Katha:** Hoot sir parade lo shapes oka rule tho nadustunnayi. Next evaru?
**Ela aadali:** Shapes line lo untayi (🔴🔵🔴🔵…). Next ye shape ravalo choose cheyyali.
**Levels:** rules: repeat → perugutunna count → rotation → colour cycle → mixed rules.
**Score:** correct × combo + speed.
**Funny:** wrong aithe parade lo shapes anni okadanini okati dhee kodatayi.
**Nerchukunedi:** patterns ardham chesukovadam.

### 17. Odd Blob Out — Zib

**Ela aadali:** 4–12 blobs untayi. Okati rule ki match avvadu (colour, shape, kallu, topi). Daanni tap cheyyali.
**Levels:** blobs perugutayi, pai levels lo rendu attributes kalipi rules.
**Score:** correct × combo.
**Funny:** odd blob pattubadithe "eh, nenu normal ne!" ani argue chestundi.
**Nerchukunedi:** grouping, classification.

### 18. Balance Buddies — Prof. Hoot
**Katha:** Friends see-saw meeda koorchunnaru. Evaru ekkuva barvu?
**Ela aadali:** See-saws lo comparisons chupistayi (Bonbon > Pip, Pip > Zib…). Andarilo ekkuva barvu (leda takkuva barvu) evaro tap cheyyali.
**Levels:** buddies 3 → 7, konni comparisons hide, "lightest" questions kooda.
**Score:** correct × combo.
**Funny:** Bonbon koorchunte see-saw ekkada lekunda galilo egurutundi.
**Nerchukunedi:** reasoning (A > B, B > C ante A > C).

# ⚡ SPEED GAMES (4)

### 19. Whack-a-Gloop — Gloop, Momo *(run, 3 hearts)*
**Katha:** Gloops garden lo nunchi bayataki vastunnaru. Bonk cheyyandi — kaani Momo ni kottakandi!
**Ela aadali:** Holes nunchi Gloops pop avutaru → tap chesi bonk cheyyandi. **Momo** vasthe tap cheyyakandi (heart pothundi). **Golden Gloop** = big bonus.
**Levels:** holes 6 → 12, tondaraga pop, decoys.
**Score:** prathi bonk ki 10 × combo.
**Funny:** bonk aina Gloop pancake laaga flat ayyi malli pop avutundi.
**Nerchukunedi:** reaction speed + control.

### 20. Turbo Dash — Turbo *(run, 3 hearts)*
**Katha:** Slowest snail, fastest rocket! Turbo race lo unnadu.
**Ela aadali:** 3 lanes. Swipe (phone) leda ← → (keyboard) tho lane maarchi cones, puddles avoid cheyyali, leaves collect cheyyali.
**Levels:** speed eppudu perugutune untundi, kotha obstacles.
**Score:** distance + collect chesinavi.
**Funny:** crash aithe Turbo shell lo daakkuni spin avutadu.
**Nerchukunedi:** fast decisions.

### 21. Bake Drop — Bonbon *(run, 3 hearts)*
**Katha:** Bonbon paina nunchi cake ingredients visurutundi — bowl lo pattali!
**Ela aadali:** Bowl ni left/right kadilinchi strawberries, chocolate, cream pattali. **Chilli peppers** avoid cheyyali (heart pothundi).
**Levels:** tondaraga padatayi, wind, pedda combos.
**Score:** prathi catch ki points × combo.
**Funny:** chilli padithe Bonbon nooti nunchi fire vastundi 🌶️🔥
**Nerchukunedi:** hand-eye coordination.

### 22. Quick Draw — Prof. Hoot
**Katha:** Hoot sir "NOW!" ani arustaru. Evaru fastest?
**Ela aadali:** Wait… wait… Hoot "NOW!" ani cheppina ventane tap cheyyali. Mundu tap chesthe foul. Fake cues ("NOW-ish", "NO!", wrong colour) kooda vastayi.
**Levels:** reaction window chinnadi avutundi, fake cues perugutayi.
**Score:** entha fast ga tap chesthe anni points (milliseconds lo chupistundi).
**Funny:** fake cue ki tap chesthe Hoot sir "Hoo-hoo-hoo!" ani navvutaru.
**Nerchukunedi:** reaction speed + patience.

# 📺 YOUTUBE SHORTS VERSION — "Comment lo answer cheppandi"

Avunu, 30 games anni YouTube videos ki set avutayi. Momo Shuffle videos laage prathi game ki **Video Mode** untundi: same game code nunchi 9:16 Short automatic ga render avutundi.

## Video flow (prathi Short, 20–40 seconds)
1. **Hook (0–2s):** "Only 3% can spot him 👀" laanti big text + character.
2. **Challenge:** game play avutundi (fish swim, Kranky steal, maze…).
3. **Freeze:** "COMMENT YOUR ANSWER 👇" + options ki simple labels: **1 / 2 / 3** leda **A / B / C**.
4. **Think timer (5–10s):** tick-tock music, character tease.
5. **End card:** "Play unlimited levels → momoblobs.com" + **level code** (e.g. `FISH-4821`).
6. **Answer reveal ledu** — 24 hours tarvata pinned comment lo answer (Momo Shuffle laage → comments, rewatch ekkuva).

## Rules — comments easy ga undali
- Answer eppudu **chinnadi**: number (1–9), letter (A–D), colour, leda character peru. Pedda answers vaddu.
- Prathi video ki **answer key + pinned comment + title/description/hashtags** automatic ga generate avutayi (ippudu unna `answers_PRIVATE.md` laage).
- **Level code:** video lo chupinche code website lo enter chesthe **same challenge** aadachu → YouTube nunchi website ki traffic.
- **Daily Challenge:** roju Short lo unna level ne website lo "Today's challenge" ga untundi.
