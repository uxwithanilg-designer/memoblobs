> Inspiration: a Minion Rush gameplay reel (3-lane runner on a seaside boardwalk at dusk: auto-run, swipe to change
> lane / jump / roll, banana lines, oncoming carts with drivers, barrels, a disco-ball shield, a magnet aura, trick
> bonuses like "Jump over +132", a mission counter and a score multiplier). We keep the *feel* of that loop — one
> thumb, fast, "one more run" — and build our own game with the MOMOBLOBS cast, our worlds and new mechanics.
> No minions, no bananas, no copied names, characters, levels or art.

**Pitch (one line):** Momo dashes through 12 worlds chasing the Grumpies who stole the Rainbow Jelly — squish under
bars, roll into a ball, split into three, and grow a conga line of friends behind you — in a runner whose stages
never run out and never feel the same.

## 1. Core loop (the first 10 seconds)

1. Momo runs forward by itself on a 3-lane track; the world bends away over the horizon (curved-world look).
2. **Swipe left/right** = change lane. **Swipe up** = boing jump. **Swipe down** = squish flat (slide under bars;
   in the air it slams Momo down). Keyboard: arrows / WASD. Tap = use the stored power.
3. Grab coins, dodge barriers, carts and Grumpies, hit power-ups.
4. A **side bump** = stumble (Momo wobbles, the Grumpy Guard pops up behind; a second stumble within 8 s = caught).
   A **head-on crash** = the last friend in the conga saves Momo, or the run ends.
5. Stage mode: reach the **finish gate** (600 m at stage 1 → ~1800 m late; runs of 1–2 minutes). Endless: as far
   as you can.

## 2. The big ideas (what makes it ours, and new)

### 2.1 Squish & Morph — Momo is clay
Momo's body is the gameplay. Besides squish (slide) and boing (jump), **Morph Gates** on the track change Momo for
a few seconds:
- **Ball** — roll fast, smash crates and barrels, bounce off walls into the next lane.
- **Split** — Momo splits into **3 mini-Momos, one per lane**: collect a whole row; one mini lost is fine, all three
  must not be.
- **Stretch** — tall and bendy: reach high coin arcs, but squish is needed under every bar.
- **Giant** — huge Momo crushes carts and trams for a big bonus (the "power fantasy" moment).
Each morph has its own sound and look. Later stages ask you to *choose* the right gate (two gates side by side).

### 2.2 Gang Conga — friends run behind you
Friend bubbles (Pip, Zib, Bolt, Gloop, Bonbon, Hoot, Turbo, Noodle, Tiki, Kranky) — touch one and the friend
joins a **conga line** behind Momo (up to 5), swaying lane to lane after you.
- **Each friend = one save:** on a crash the last friend tumbles off with a "hero bump" and Momo keeps going.
- **Each friend lends a power while in line:** Zib = coin magnet, Pip = slows carts, Bolt = higher jump,
  Gloop = sticky (no stumbles), Bonbon = cake bomb clears the lane ahead every 15 s, Hoot = shows the safe lane
  early, Turbo = faster lane switches, Noodle = longer morphs, Tiki = coin ×2 on coin melodies, Kranky = snips
  Grumpies.
- **Conga Fever:** 5 friends for 10 s → the whole line glows, score ×2, music gets a party layer.

### 2.3 Villain Chase
Every world has a villain who stole a piece of the Rainbow Jelly and runs ahead on a vehicle, throwing obstacles
back onto the track. Normal stages end at the gate; **chase stages** end when you catch the thief (hit 3 glowing
bump pads that launch Momo at the vehicle). Stage 10 = mid-boss, stage 20 = world boss with a 3-phase chase.

### 2.4 Portal choice (fork in the road)
Twice a stage the track splits: **left portal = Coin Lane** (easy, lots of coins), **right portal = Star Lane**
(harder, a star piece and big bonus). The camera does a white "whoosh" and you land in a short themed zone
(water slide, rollercoaster, zipline, sky bubble flight, ceiling run in space) before rejoining. Every run has
choices — two players never play the same stage the same way.

### 2.5 Coin Melody
Coins in a line play rising notes of the world's song; finishing a full line plays the phrase with a drum fill
and gives a **Melody bonus**. The music the player hears is partly made by their path (Momo Boing's best idea,
made lighter).

### 2.6 Trick bonuses (the "+132 Jump over" feeling)
Pop-up bonuses with a slow-mo flash: **Jump Over** (a cart), **Close Call** (switch lane just before a crash),
**Squish Slide** (under a bar at the last moment), **Grumpy Bonk** (land on a Grumpy — it pops into confetti),
**Rail Grind**, **Rooftop Run** (run on a tram roof end to end). A combo meter chains them.

### Stage types (rotate through each world)
Dash (reach the gate) · Coin Collector (collect N) · Friend Rescue (free caged friends) · Thief Chase · Time Trial ·
Perfect Run (no hits for 3 stars) · Morph Madness · Rail Rush (rails, trams, roofs) · Letter Hunt (spell
M-O-M-O-B-L-O-B-S along the stage) · Boss Chase.
**Stars:** 1 = reach the gate, 2 = the stage goal, 3 = goal with no crash.

## 4. The stage algorithm (unlimited, harder, never feels repeated)

### 4.1 Chunk library
The track is built from **chunks**: hand-made 3-lane patterns 20–60 m long, written in a tiny text format (one row
= 2 m, read bottom-up):
```
// .=empty o=coin J=low barrier (jump) S=high bar (squish) B=block (switch) C=oncoming cart
// T=tram  R=ramp  _=gap  G=Grumpy  P=power-up  F=friend bubble  M=morph gate
"o.B", "o.B", "o..", "J.o", "..o", "S.o", "C.."
```
Each chunk has tags: **tier 1–6**, skills (jump, squish, switch, rooftop, morph, rails, gap), length, safe entry
and exit lanes, and which worlds can use it. Target: ~60 chunks in Phase 1, ~180 by Phase 2.

### 4.2 Mutators (one chunk → many looks)
Mirror · lane rotate · reskin (a barrier becomes a candy fence, a snow wall, a lava pipe…) · moving variant (a
static block becomes an oncoming cart) · density thin/thicken · coin pattern swap (line, arc, zigzag, letters,
melody) · overlay two easy chunks into one harder one · speed pad insert.
180 chunks × mirror × 3 lanes × 4 coin patterns × moving variants → **thousands of different-feeling sections**.

### 4.3 Stage shape (a grammar, not random noise)
`Warm-up → Build → Breather → Build → Spotlight → Finale`
- *Warm-up*: tier ≤ 2, coins, no hard choices.
- *Build*: chunks chosen by a difficulty budget.
- *Breather*: a coin feast, rail ride or portal zone — players rest before the next push.
- *Spotlight*: the stage type's special thing (morph gates, a rescue cage, the thief's barrage…).
- *Finale*: the gate or the boss chase.
Stages are seeded by stage number: a retry plays the **same** stage (learnable, ghost-race friendly); Endless is
fresh every run.

### 4.4 Fairness check (no impossible moments)
Every generated stage runs through a **validator**: a small search over (row, lane, height state) with the reaction
time the current speed allows. If no clean path exists, the chunk is rerolled. A second rule caps how many
decisions per second a stage asks for. Then a **par bot** and a careless **kid bot** play it in balance sweeps
(like Momo Boing's `boing_sweep.mjs`).

### 4.5 Difficulty curve
`D(n)` = a per-world **sawtooth** (rises across the world, dips at the start of the next) + a slow **log** growth
over all stages. D drives:
- run speed: 8.5 m/s at stage 1 → ~14 m/s at stage 240 → creeps to ~15.5 m/s later (Endless goes to 18);
- chunk tier mix and obstacle density;
- reaction window (rows between threats);
- moving things (cart speed, two-way traffic, geyser timing);
- power-up and friend frequency (fewer later, never zero).
Target win rates (par + kid bots together): ~85% early → ~62% at stage 240 → slowly lower after, like Momo Boing.
A **quiet helper**: after 3 losses on a stage it gets a little kinder (slower start, one more friend bubble);
nothing on screen says so.

### 4.6 Freshness memory (why it never feels repeated)
- The game remembers (on the device) the last ~80 chunk+mutator signatures and the last 12 set pieces you saw.
  Recently seen pieces get a lower weight; a set piece never comes back within 6 stages (shuffle bag).
- **Novelty quota:** every stage must contain at least one thing you have not seen in the last 5 stages (a set
  piece, a morph, a chunk family, a portal zone).
- Even the coin lines and scenery props rotate, so the *look* changes as much as the layout.

### 4.7 The Remix Loop (stage 241 → ∞)
After stage 240 the stages keep coming, one by one, built from everything above plus remix layers:
- **World blends:** track and obstacles from one world, scenery and villain from another (12 worlds → 66 pairs,
  shuffled per cycle, the same main world never twice in a row).
- **Time of day:** day, morning, sunset, night (headlight + lanterns), aurora.
- **Weather:** snow, rain puddles, fireflies, leaves, embers, stars, bubbles.
- **Twists** (sometimes): mirror world, speed burst, night run, low gravity, fog, coin rain, two-lane bridge
  stretches.
- **Generated names** ("Frosty Boardwalk", "Neon Jungle"…) and **music remixes** (the beat of one world, the lead
  of another).
66 pairs × 5 skies × 7 weathers × twists — **tens of thousands of combinations** before an exact look repeats, and
the chunk layer underneath is different every time.

## 5. Modes
- **Stages** — one by one, no stage select (like Momo Boing): the next stage is saved on the device; leaving mid-run
  restarts that stage.
- **Endless Dash** — the classic score run: speed rises forever, missions raise your multiplier. Opens at stage 6.
- **Daily Dash** — one seeded run per day, the same for everyone, with a streak bonus.
- **Ghost race** — your best try at the current stage runs beside you as a see-through Momo.

## 6. Power-ups, missions and the shop
- **Power-ups on the track:** Coin Magnet, Bubble Shield (the disco-ball moment — our version is a bubble with
  music notes), Sky Bubble (float above the track through a coin trail), Super Boing shoes (big jumps),
  Score ×2, Turbo Dash (invincible speed ride for 4 s).
- **Jelly Board:** a hoverboard-style board you can start a run with (double-tap): saves one crash and lets you
  jump higher for 30 s. Earned from stars and the Daily Dash.
- **Missions:** 3 at a time ("Jump over 10 carts", "Collect 300 coins in one run", "Ride 3 trams"); finishing a set
  raises the score multiplier (×1 → ×20).
- **Shop (site coins):** play as other MOMOBLOBS friends (each with a small passive), hats (Blob Brigade's hat
  models), trails, boards, power-up upgrades (longer magnet, longer shield).

## 7. Look and sound
- 3D clay look like our other games; **curved-world** vertex bend so the track rolls over the horizon; strong
  depth: near props (lamps, benches, stalls), mid buildings, far skyline and sky gradient; fog per world.
- Camera behind and above Momo, a slight swing on lane changes, a slow-mo flash on trick bonuses.
- Momo runs with a squishy waddle; the conga line sways after Momo.
- Music: one calm + one action loop per world (Momo Boing / Blob Brigade studio), coin melody notes in the world's
  key, punchy SFX (whoosh, boing, squish, crash, morph pops).

## 8. Tech plan
- `src/dash/` mirrors `src/boing/`: pure-TS `sim.ts` (lanes, jump/squish physics, collisions, morphs, conga,
  par/kid bots), `chunks.ts` (the chunk library in the text format), `levels.ts` (D(n), stage grammar, mutators,
  freshness, remix), `validate.ts`, three.js `render.ts`/`models.ts` (reuse Momo and friends from
  `src/boing/models.ts`, villains and hats from `src/crowd/models.ts`), `session.ts`, `save.ts`, `ui/DashApp.tsx`.
- Instanced meshes and recycled chunks for steady 60 fps on phones; automatic quality.
- Scripts: `scripts/dash_sweep.mjs`, `scripts/dash_tune.mjs`, `scripts/gen_dash_audio.py`,
  `scripts/build_dash_art.py`. Dev hook `window.__dash` (autopilot, debugAdvance).

## 9. Phases
- **Phase 1:** core runner (3 lanes, jump, squish, stumble/crash, curved world), chunk library ~60, validator, bots,
  worlds 1–2 (Sunset Pier, Candy Town) = stages 1–40, Coin Magnet / Bubble Shield / Score ×2 / Sky Bubble,
  Gang Conga with 3 friends, trick bonuses, coin melodies, Endless Dash, music + SFX for 2 worlds, title / results /
  settings, one-by-one stage save, mobile controls.
- **Phase 2:** worlds 3–12 (stages 41–240), all 4 morphs, trams/rooftops, rails, set pieces and portal zones,
  villain chases and bosses, all 10 friends, missions + multiplier, Daily Dash, Jelly Board, balance sweeps.
- **Phase 3:** the Remix Loop (241 → ∞) with freshness memory, the quiet helper, ghost race, shop (characters,
  hats, trails, upgrades), final tuning.
