> Inspiration: endless jungle runners and fruit-collecting adventures in general — we make our own: an original hero,
> an original forest world and story, and new mechanics. No borrowed characters, names, levels or art (no ties, no
> barrel cannons, no mine carts — our own set pieces).

**Pitch (one line):** Kiko the monkey races through 300 places of a living forest — over branches, along vines, down
waterfalls and across rivers — collecting bananas and fruits to win back the 15 Golden Seeds and bring the Great Fruit
Tree back to life.

## 1. Story

**The Great Fruit Tree** stands in the middle of the forest. Every morning it drops fruit for every animal. One night
**Baron Bramble** — a grumpy thorn-bush wizard who hates sharing — and his **Grumpy gang** steal the tree's **15 Golden
Seeds** and hide them across the forest. The tree starts to wilt; the fruit stops falling.

**Kiko**, a cheeky little monkey who loves bananas more than anything, wakes up to an empty fruit bowl — and sets off.
With him flies **Pico**, a chatty toucan who gives hints (and bad jokes). Along the way Kiko helps the animals of each
region; each region's guardian animal becomes a ride, and each region ends at a Grumpy boss who guards one Golden Seed.

- **Act 1 (regions 1–5):** "The Wilting Forest" — Kiko finds out what happened and wins the first five seeds.
- **Act 2 (regions 6–10):** "The Storm Season" — Baron Bramble sends rain, night and fog to stop Kiko.
- **Act 3 (regions 11–15):** "The Thorn Castle" — the long road to the Baron's castle on the volcano.
- **Stage 300 — The Great Fruit Tree:** Kiko plants all 15 seeds; the tree blooms; a fruit rain falls on the whole
  forest; even Baron Bramble gets a banana (and smiles for the first time).
- **After 300 — The Wild Forest:** the tree's new seeds grow new places every day — the run never ends.

Little story beats between regions (short comic cards, 3–4 panels, skippable), funny lines from Pico, and the MOMOBLOBS
gang visiting (Momo turns up in Banana Grove and becomes a playable runner).

## 2. Kiko and the controls

- **Kiko:** an orange-brown clay monkey with a big curly tail, a banana-shaped tuft and huge eyes; clearcoat shine;
  expressive faces (grin, surprise, "yuck" for rotten fruit, burp after a fruit feast).
- **Swipe left / right** — change lane. **Swipe up** — jump (double jump when a leaf-wing power is on).
  **Swipe down** — slide / roll. **Tap** — grab a vine, a branch or a zipline when one is close (a ring shows when).
  Keyboard: arrows/WASD, Space to grab.
- **Two heights of path:** the forest floor and the **branch road** above it. Vines, ramps of roots and bouncy
  mushrooms take Kiko up; jumping off takes Kiko down. The high road is harder and has more fruit.

## 4. A stage (big, 2–3 minutes, with checkpoints)

Every stage is built from five parts, each ending at a **banana-flag checkpoint**:

1. **Warm-up run** — the location's look, a few fruits, one easy hazard.
2. **Set piece** — the stage's big moment: vine swings, a raft ride, a waterfall slide, an animal ride, a collapsing
   bridge, a bee chase, a lily-pad crossing, a zipline, a cliff dive, a boat in the caves…
3. **Challenge** — the location's hazards together, faster; the **high branch road** and the **low road** split here.
4. **Secret** — a hidden fruit room behind a waterfall, a leaf pile or a hollow log (a **Golden Banana** inside).
5. **Finale** — a sprint to the **Fruit Gate**, a chase, or (every 20th stage) the region boss.

**Stars:** reach the gate · find the 3 Golden Bananas · fill the stage's fruit basket (e.g. "12 mangoes").
Stages get longer as you go (≈ 1:45 at the start → ≈ 3:00 later), with more set pieces per stage (1 → 3).

### 25 set pieces (mixed per stage)
Vine swing chain · zipline · rope bridge (collapses behind you) · log raft rapids · lily-pad hops · crocodile-back hops ·
waterfall slide · cliff dive into a pool · bouncy mushroom chain · bamboo catapult · leaf glide (Pico) · elephant
stampede ride · turtle river ride · squirrel sled downhill · snow sled · glowworm boat in a cave · mudslide · rising
water climb · bee-swarm chase · boulder roll chase · kite ride on the wind · geyser launches · lava stepping stones ·
whirlpool ring · tree-trunk spiral climb.

## 5. Making 300 places that never feel the same — then forever

Each stage gets a **concept card** built from parts, checked so that neighbours never share the same main parts:
**location** (one of 300 names) + **time** (dawn, day, sunset, night) + **weather** (sun, rain, storm, fog, wind, snow,
ash, blossom) + **set pieces** (1–3 of 25) + **hazard set** (the region's own + two older ones) + **featured fruit** +
**route shape** (floor only, branch road, split routes, water section) + **secret type** + **goal**.

- Inside a stage the track is built from hand-made **chunks** (like Momo Dash) — about 200 for the forest floor, the
  branch road and the water — mirrored, re-skinned for the region and mixed with fruit patterns.
- A **freshness memory** keeps chunks and set pieces seen lately rare; a set piece never repeats within 3 stages.
- **After stage 300 — the Wild Forest:** every day the tree "grows" new places: two regions blended (the look of one,
  the hazards of another), a season (spring, monsoon, autumn, winter), weather and a twist (night, fog, double speed,
  low water, fruit rain), with generated names ("Misty Bamboo Falls", "Frozen Mango Bend") — thousands of combinations.
- **Difficulty:** speed, hazard density, reaction time, the number of set pieces and the share of the high road go up
  with the stage number in waves (each region starts a bit easier than the last one ended). Bosses and every 10th
  stage are the hard peaks; the stage after a boss is a breather.

## 6. Lives (hearts)

- **Start with 3 hearts.** A hazard costs one heart and sends Kiko back to the last banana flag.
- **Get hearts back:** heart-shaped **Heart Fruit** (rare, often on the hard high road or in secrets), **150 bananas =
  +1 heart**, beating a boss refills hearts, and the Daily Jungle Hunt gives one.
- **More hearts as you go:** the heart limit grows with progress — 3 → 4 (stage 25) → 5 (50) → 6 (100) → 7 (175) →
  8 (250). Later stages are harder, but Kiko can carry more hearts into them.
- **0 hearts:** a funny "Kiko faints into a banana pile" screen — the run is over and the next run starts again at
  Banana Grove (hearts carry through the whole run, so the heart limit and Heart Fruits matter a lot).
- A **Coconut Helmet** power-up absorbs one hit without losing a heart.

## 7. Don't touch! (hazards)

Thorny bushes · poison mushrooms (purple, spotty) · **rotten fruit** (brown, buzzing flies — eat one and Kiko gets a
funny "yuck" wobble and loses the fruit streak) · bee hives · snakes on branches · snapping fly-trap plants · falling
coconuts (a shadow warns first) · spiky durians · porcupines · skunk clouds (blur the screen for a moment) · spider webs
(stick for a second) · crocodile jaws · piranha splashes · quicksand · mud (slow) · lightning puddles in storms ·
falling stalactites · icicles · lava stones · banana peels (Kiko slips and slides — a trick if you jump in time!) ·
Grumpy goons throwing things.

## 8. Fruits and power-ups

- **Bananas** are the coins (and 150 = a heart). **Fruits** (mango, pineapple, berries, watermelon, coconut, dragonfruit)
  fill the **Smoothie meter**. **Golden Bananas** (3 per stage) are the stars.
- **Fruit Salad:** five different fruits in a row → a big bonus and a happy Kiko dance.
- **Smoothie Rush:** a full Smoothie meter → 6–9 seconds of super speed, invincible, with a rainbow trail.
- **Power-ups:** Banana Magnet, Coconut Helmet (one free hit), Leaf Wings (glide over gaps, double jump), Pepper Rocket
  (6–9 s turbo), Bubble Float (cross water), Bounce Shoes.
- **Animal rides** (each region's guardian): smash through hazards for a while.

## 9. Funny on purpose

Kiko slips on peels, burps after a Fruit Salad, pulls faces at rotten fruit, gets stuck in webs with a squeak, and does
a victory banana dance at the gate. Pico comments ("That bee looked angry. Was it angry? It was angry."). Bosses are
silly: the Croc King has a crown that keeps slipping, the Storm Grump sneezes lightning, Baron Bramble trips on his own
thorns.

## 10. Around the game

- **Kiko's Treehouse:** spend bananas to decorate and grow the treehouse (hammocks, swings, fruit stands) — it shows
  how far Kiko has come.
- **Sticker album:** every new animal or place met becomes a sticker (300 places + 60 animals).
- **Daily Jungle Hunt:** a daily stage from the Wild Forest with a fruit goal and a streak.
- **Wardrobe:** Kiko hats, tail ribbons, trails; Momo and friends as guest runners.
- **Music:** one theme per region (marimba for Banana Grove, steel drums for Mango River, flutes for Bamboo Valley,
  harp for Waterfall Cliffs, bassoon-and-pizzicato for Mushroom Hollow, rain percussion for Monsoon Marsh…), a chase
  version for bosses, and a jingle for Smoothie Rush. Every fruit has its own pop sound; bananas play a little scale.

## 11. Look and feel (same bar as Momo Dash)

Glossy clay characters, a living forest in layers (ferns and flowers up close, giant trunks and rivers in the middle,
misty mountains and waterfalls far away), god rays through the leaves, rain and fog, fireflies at night, water that
shines, mobile-first and app-like.

## 12. Build plan

Built on the Momo Dash engine (chunks, track generator, look-ahead bot, world switching renderer, bend, bloom), plus
the new parts: the branch road (a second height), grab-and-swing, water modes, hearts and checkpoints, weather physics,
animal rides, set pieces.

- **Phase 1:** Kiko (model and animations), the core run from Banana Grove (lanes, jump, slide, grab, high and low
  roads), bananas, fruits, Smoothie Rush, hearts and banana-flag checkpoints, the first hazards, regions 1–2 (stages 1–40) with vine swings,
  rope bridges, log rafts and lily pads, the story intro, music for 2 regions, mobile UI.
- **Phase 2:** regions 3–15 (stages 41–300), all set pieces, weather, animal rides, bosses, Pico's hints, Treehouse and
  sticker album, music for all regions.
- **Phase 3:** the Wild Forest (endless), the Daily Jungle Hunt, freshness memory across days, the wardrobe, the
  stage-300 finale, balance sweeps across all 300 stages. (Built — see §12c.)

## 13. How a run works (decided: start from the beginning)

The user chose **no saved stage progress** — every visit (and every new run) starts at **Kiko's Treehouse in Banana
Grove**, like Momo Dash:
- The stages flow into each other without stopping (no stage numbers on screen, no stage select); the location name
  shows as Kiko enters each place, and a region gate with a white flash leads into the next region.
- Banana-flag checkpoints still matter inside a run: losing a heart sends Kiko back to the last flag. When the hearts
  run out the run ends — "Kiko faints into a banana pile" — and the next run starts at Banana Grove again.
- The heart limit grows with how far the current run has gone (stage 25, 50, 100, 175, 250), so a long run gets
  sturdier as it gets harder.
- What is kept on the device: best distance and score, the furthest region reached, bananas spent in the Treehouse,
  the sticker album, the wardrobe, missions and settings — never the stage.
- Because later regions take a long run to reach, the **Daily Jungle Hunt** starts each day's run in a different
  region (the same for everyone), so every player gets to visit the whole forest.
- Region stages stay big, but in a run each stage is ≈ 60–90 s of new place, so a strong run passes a region every
  ~20–25 minutes and the stage-300 finale is a true champion run.
